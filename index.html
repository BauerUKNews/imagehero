Image Hero — Help Guide
Everything you need to create great news images
1
Choose your canvas size
Before uploading, pick the format you need.
🖥Landscape 16:9 — standard news hero, exports 1920×1080px
📱Mobile 9:16 — portrait for phones, exports 1080×1920px
⬛Square 1:1 — social media, exports 1080×1080px
2
Upload your image
Click Choose Image or drag and drop a photo onto the canvas. Once uploaded the foreground and background controls unlock.
3
Position the foreground
Drag the image directly on the canvas to reposition it. Use the Scale slider to make it larger or smaller. Horizontal and Vertical sliders give precise control.
4
Crop (optional)
Click Draw Crop Area then drag a rectangle on the canvas. Drag the corner handles to adjust. Click ✓ Confirm Crop to apply — this permanently trims the image.
5
Generate Background
Click Generate Background to fill the canvas with a blurred, dimmed version of your image. Adjust with:
Blur — how blurry the background is
Dim — how dark the background overlay is
Pan H / Pan V — shift the background behind the foreground
Vignette — adds a gradient darkening to edges — great for text legibility. Choose Bottom to darken the bottom of the image.
Switching to Multi Image mode
Click Multi Image in the top bar. The upload section changes to accept multiple files at once.
1
Upload multiple images
Click Choose Images and select 2 or more photos. You can also add more later. Images appear in a list with thumbnails.
2
Choose a layout
Layout options appear automatically based on how many images you've uploaded. The mini preview shows how the canvas will be divided.
2 images: BG + Circle overlay, Side by Side, Top & Bottom, Big Left/Right
3 images: Stacked, Thirds, Big+Two, Two+Big, Big Top
4 images: Stacked, Quarters, Four Across, Big+3 combos
5+ images: Stacked strips or auto grid
3
Reorder images
Each image in the list has ▲ ▼ buttons — click to move it up or down in the order. You can also click and drag any row to a new position. The canvas updates instantly.
4
Adjust each panel
Click a panel on the canvas (or click its row in the list) to select it. Then:
Drag — pan the image within its panel
Shift + Drag — drag up to zoom in, drag down to zoom out
Zoom / Pan sliders — fine control in the Selected Panel section
For circle overlays, extra Circle Size & Position sliders appear so you can resize and move the circle anywhere on the canvas.
5
Background & Vignette
The Background section works the same as in Single Image mode. Add a vignette to darken the edges — useful when text will be placed over the image.
1
Export your image
Click ↓ Export HD Image at any time — you don't need to complete every step first. A naming dialog will appear.
2
Name your file — SEO matters
You must name the file before downloading. Search engines read image filenames, so use descriptive, hyphenated keywords.
✓ Good: rishi-sunak-budget-press-conference.jpg
✗ Avoid: IMG_4821.jpg or hero image.jpg
3
Export sizes
🖥Landscape — 1920 × 1080 px, JPEG 92%
📱Mobile — 1080 × 1920 px, JPEG 92%
⬛Square — 1080 × 1080 px, JPEG 92%
Reset
Click ✕ Reset to clear everything and start again. Canvas size goes back to Landscape.
